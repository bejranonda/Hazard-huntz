Extreme heat: keeping everyone at home safe from heatstroke

Get ready

  1. Look after elders, young children, pregnant women, the ill
    Sources: Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025) · Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025) · Department of Health (DOH) (2023) · World Health Organization (WHO) (2026) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026) · U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) (2026)
  2. Follow heat warnings and the heat index
    Sources: Bangkok Metropolitan Administration (BMA) (2026) · Thai Meteorological Department (TMD) (2024)
  3. Keep water by elders; have them sip before they feel thirsty
    Sources: Department of Health (DOH) (2022) · Thai Meteorological Department (TMD) (2024) · Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025)
  4. Set up a cool, airy room for elders to rest in
    Sources: Department of Health (DOH) (2022) · Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025) · Department of Health (DOH) (2023) · World Health Organization (WHO) (2026)

Very hot days

  1. Wear light-coloured, loose clothes that breathe
    Sources: Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025) · Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025) · Department of Health (DOH) (2022) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026)
  2. Move outdoor activity to the morning or evening
    Sources: Department of Health (DOH) (2023) · Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025) · World Health Organization (WHO) (2026) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026)
  3. Avoid alcohol on very hot days
    Sources: Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025) · Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025) · Department of Health (DOH) (2022) · U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) (2026)
  4. Never leave a child, or anyone, in a car parked in the sun
    Sources: Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025) · Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025) · Department of Health (DOH) (2023) · World Health Organization (WHO) (2026) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026)

Heatstroke

  1. Very hot, not sweating, confused? Call 1669 at once
    Sources: Department of Health (DOH) (2023) · Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025) · Department of Health (DOH) (2022) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026)
  2. Into the shade, loosen clothes, wipe with a cool wet cloth
    Sources: Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025) · Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025) · Department of Health (DOH) (2026) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026)

Thai emergency helplines

  • 1784 DDPM disaster hotline: Report disasters, ask for help, 24 h
  • 199 Fire: A fire you can't put out: get out, then call the fire service
  • 1669 Medical emergency (NIEMS): Ambulance, 24 h
  • 1555 Bangkok (BMA): Report problems and get help in Bangkok
  • 1130 MEA electricity: Bangkok, Nonthaburi, Samut Prakan
  • 1129 PEA electricity: The other 74 provinces
  • 1323 Mental health hotline: Stressed or worn out after the flood? Free, 24 h
