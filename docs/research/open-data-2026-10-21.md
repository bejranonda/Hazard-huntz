## 5. What FloodWatch had already learned

Read from FloodWatch's own documents and code on 21 Oct 2026 (the owner's other project, [flood.autobahn.bot](https://flood.autobahn.bot); its repository is private). Its hosting, its route to Thai-only hosts and its private notes are left out on purpose; so are its file paths.

### 5a. How it accepts a source

- **The evidence rule:** every endpoint, field or number in its docs needs a live call (date and host), observed data, or a citation; anything else is marked. Sample output comes from running code.
- **Status marks:** live, documentation only, partial, no machine access, key needed, and **refuted** (the endpoint doesn't exist or errors: don't use it). A source is "approved" only after a call from its production host with its honest User-Agent, with the response kept as a test fixture. Every probe records its UTC window and host.
- **Research is input, not truth:** each testable claim from research is marked confirmed, refuted, unverified or an idea, by live calls, 30 days of observed data or citations; whole research files are graded reliable, mixed or do-not-use; only confirmed claims reach its docs. Its checks can be re-run by a script that prints "got" against "expected" and asks for the host's country.
- **Onboarding, five steps:** (1) a row with datum, units, timezone, licence and polling interval; (2) the terms read, or permission asked; (3) an adapter: fetch → archive raw → parse → quality checks → health; (4) two or three archived responses as fixtures, with tests for missing-value codes and timezones; (5) backfill, then the quirks recorded.
- **Tested, then dropped when they added nothing:** GloFAS discharge, daily gauge rain, Google Flood Hub (hit rate 0.20).

### 5b. How it fetches

- **One worker loop, never faster than the source:** HII levels every 10 min, rain every 15 min, Open-Meteo hourly, dams every 6 h, RID every 1–3 h. Nothing upstream is fetched on a visitor's request; browsers call only its own API.
- **The HTTP client:** User-Agent `BKK-FloodWatch/0.2 (+https://flood.autobahn.bot)`, gzip; connect timeout 10 s, read 120 s, total 240 s; 3 attempts with 1–2–4 s backoff, 5xx retried; a host that refuses connections is skipped for 10 min; **API keys only in headers**, because URLs are archived and logged.
- **A 429 is an answer:** bulk history stops at the first one. Each source has its own circuit breaker and health row; after 3 failures its interval doubles, up to ×6 (core feeds ×2).
- **Storage:** the raw body is saved before parsing, gzipped, with its SHA-256, URL, status and UTC fetch time, write-once. Times are stored in UTC; local times are parsed explicitly as +07:00.
- **Quality flags, never deletion:** |value| ≥ 1000 (the 999999 code), levels more than 3 m above the bank, times more than 15 min in the future.
- **Freshness:** a feed is stale when its newest reading is over 1 h (BMA) or 3 h (HII) old; national rows older than 3× the cadence, or dated 1970, are dropped; the page says "ไม่อัปเดต" after 3 h and "unknown" after 24 h.
- **Never assume a source's schedule:** probe a cheap timestamp first, download only a finished layer, and replace stored data only when the download has at least 95 % of the announced cells.
- **Open-Meteo quota:** research gets 3,000 weighted calls a day (1,000 an hour, 10 s apart), weight = locations × max(1, variables/10) × max(1, days/14), one shared ledger, and a test that fails any research script calling Open-Meteo without it. The free tier, as FloodWatch read it on 6 Oct 2026: 600 a minute, 5,000 an hour, 10,000 a day, non-commercial only.
- **Geo-blocks:** no browser spoofing, proxy rotation or bot-challenge solving.

### 5d. Pitfalls it recorded

- **Time:** HII levels, rain, history and FEWS are Bangkok time with no offset; the HII chart, Traffy, BMA's `/Date(ms)/` and Flood Hub are UTC; hgwrc is Thai time; DWR and RID use Buddhist-era dates, and RID's "24.00" is the next day's 00:00; a rain day runs 07:00–07:00 and HII's daily rain is labelled by the day its window ends; 28 HII gauges once arrived about 21 h in the future.
- **Datums:** HII and RID in m MSL (Ko Lak); the Navy in LLW with unknown offsets; DWR, hgwrc and RID's hourly table use local staff zeros. BMA and HII differ by 0.3–0.6 m at the same place: compare each gauge only with its own bank. BMA's `critical` is a pumping target, not a bank.
- **Missing values:** 999999; bank and ground 0 in the chart; BMA −99; RID "-" or "*"; dam level 0; GSMaP −999.9; an error inside an HTTP 200 (HII); old values repeated under new dates.
- **Rate limits:** HII returned 429 to its server's address, and again after 27 rain-history calls 20 s apart; Open-Meteo 429 for one request of 49 points × 1 year; Traffy 502 at `limit=500`.
- **CORS:** HII's api-v3 echoes the origin; Traffy and Open-Meteo send `*`; the tiwrm chart sends none.

### 5e. FloodWatch's own API, and two of its rules worth reusing

- **`/api/stations`** (free, no key, "keep requests reasonable"; `public, max-age=60, stale-while-revalidate=300`): every gauge's status, distance to the bank, 24 h change and 24/48/72 h forecast rows; no location needed; **no CORS header**. Its code is MIT; third-party data keeps its own terms; it is "not an official warning".
- **`/api/point?lat=&lon=`:** an area category with a confidence, the nearest gauges, rain and report counts, never a level at the pin. It needs the user's coordinates; FloodWatch strips the query from its access logs and blocks it in `robots.txt`. Not for this app.
- **Links into FloodWatch:** `https://flood.autobahn.bot/#p=<lat>,<lon>` or `#s=<CODE>`. The fragment isn't sent when the page loads, but FloodWatch's page then calls `/api/point` with it; a link with a position is the user's choice to make.
- **Its gauge status rule** (labelled a heuristic): at or over the bank is critical, 90 % of the bank depth or more is warning, 70 % or more is watch.
- **Rain in words,** with TMD's bands: 0.1–10, 10.1–35, 35.1–90 and 90.1 mm or more in 24 h; the nearest gauge within 10 km with a reading under 3 h old.
- **Satellite maps** say "water seen", never "not flooded".
- **The zone:** both sites are on `autobahn.bot`. Bot Fight Mode is zone-wide; when it was on (26 Sep 2026) it answered `curl` and LINE's and Facebook's preview fetchers with a challenge, and page rules couldn't skip it. It must stay off ([KNOWN_ISSUES #93](../KNOWN_ISSUES.md#93-cloudflares-bot-detection-sets-a-cookie)).
