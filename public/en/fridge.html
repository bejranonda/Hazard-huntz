Power cut: is the food in the fridge still safe?

🥛 Check the fridge

Tap how long the power has been off: the app keeps the time and shows, food by food, what in the fridge, the freezer, medicine and breast milk to throw out and what to keep. Every figure and its source is below.

🔒 The time stays on this device; nothing is sent.

How long each thing keeps, and the sources

  1. 🥛 Food in the fridge · keeps about 4 hours, if the door stays shut
    After that:
    ❌ Throw out: 🥩 Meat, fish and prawns, raw or cooked · 🌭 Meatballs, sausages, Thai pork roll, ham · 🍛 Leftover dishes: curries, soups, stir-fries, tofu · 🍚 Cooked rice, sticky rice, fresh noodles, khanom jeen · 🥚 Eggs, raw or boiled · 🥛 Fresh milk, opened milk, drinking yoghurt, yoghurt, soy milk · 🍼 Made-up baby formula · 🥭 Cut fruit and vegetables, bagged salad · 🍰 Cream cakes, cream-filled pastries, custard
    ✅ Keep, unless it is mouldy or smells, looks or feels odd: 🥕 Whole fruit and vegetables, not cut; fresh herbs · 🍞 Bread, cakes without cream · 🧀 Butter, margarine, hard cheese, cheese slices · 🍯 Soy sauce, ketchup, jam, peanut butter
    Anything not on the list: if in doubt, throw it out
    Sources: U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) (2025) · FoodSafety.gov (U.S. Department of Health and Human Services) (2024) · U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA FSIS) (2013) · U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) (2026) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
  2. ❄️ Food in the freezer · Half full: keeps about 24 hours · Full: keeps about 48 hours, if it stays shut
    After that: check each pack, as listed below
    🥩 Meat, fish and prawns: Still has ice crystals → refreeze, Fully thawed, no longer cold → throw out
    🥟 Frozen ready meals, dumplings, steamed buns: Still has ice crystals → refreeze, Fully thawed, no longer cold → throw out
    🍦 Ice cream: throw out once it starts to melt
    🍞 Bread: refreeze
    Sources: U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) (2025) · FoodSafety.gov (U.S. Department of Health and Human Services) (2024) · U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA FSIS) (2013) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
  3. 💉 Insulin · Keeps out of the fridge for up to 28 days at up to 30°C, out of the sun; never freeze it Above 30°C it slowly loses strength, but if there is no new supply yet, keep using it and replace it soon
    Sources: U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) (2026) · Siriraj Hospital (Mahidol University) · U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) (2026) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
  4. 💊 Other medicine that must be kept cold · If the power is off over a day, check the label; if it does not say it can be kept out of the fridge, ask a pharmacist before using it
    After that: if the label does not say it can be kept out of the fridge, ask a pharmacist before using it; never throw away a medicine you cannot do without, keep using it until you get a new supply
    Sources: Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026) · U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) (2026) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
  5. 🤱 Breast milk in the fridge · keeps about 4 hours, if the door stays shut
    After that: milk that is no longer cold: if in doubt, throw it out
    Sources: U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) (2026) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
  6. 🤱 Breast milk in the freezer · Half full: keeps about 24 hours · Full: keeps about 48 hours, if it stays shut
    After that: check each bag: still has ice crystals, refreeze it · fully thawed but still cold, put it in the fridge and use it within the next day, or throw it out · never refreeze thawed milk
    Sources: U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) (2026) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
  7. 🚪 Open the fridge as little as you can, so the cold lasts
    Sources: U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) (2025) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
  8. 🚫 Never taste food to check it; if in doubt, throw it out
    Sources: U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) (2025) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
  9. 🌡️ In a hot room, or with the door opened often, food keeps less long than the times above
    Sources: UK Food Standards Agency (FSA) (2023) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
  10. 🥫 Throw out cans that are dented, bulging, leaking or rusty
    Sources: Thai Food and Drug Administration (FDA) (2020) · Department of Health (DOH) (2021)
