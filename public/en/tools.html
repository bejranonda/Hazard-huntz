📋 What to do in a disaster

📲 Turn on emergency alerts on your phone

DDPM sends warnings to every phone in an area at risk: no sign-up and no internet needed, on iPhones with iOS 18 or later and Android 11 or later

iPhone

  1. Settings
  2. Notifications
  3. Scroll to the very bottom
  4. Turn on Emergency Alerts (or TH-ALERT) and Amber Alerts

Android

  1. Settings
  2. Safety and emergency
  3. Emergency alerts (on some phones: Wireless emergency alerts)
  4. Turn on "Allow alerts", and every kind of alert

Menu names differ a little by make and model

  • 🔊 It sounds for about 8 seconds, even on silent, locked or on vibrate
  • 🔗 Real messages come from DDPM and have no link to tap
  • 📱 Also follow the THAI DISASTER ALERT app and DDPM’s LINE account for 1784

Thai emergency helplines

  • 1784 DDPM disaster hotline: Report disasters, ask for help, 24 h
  • 199 Fire: A fire you can't put out: get out, then call the fire service
  • 1669 Medical emergency (NIEMS): Ambulance, 24 h
  • 1555 Bangkok (BMA): Report problems and get help in Bangkok
  • 1130 MEA electricity: Bangkok, Nonthaburi, Samut Prakan
  • 1129 PEA electricity: The other 74 provinces
  • 1323 Mental health hotline: Stressed or worn out after the flood? Free, 24 h
