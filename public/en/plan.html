Family plan card: if something happens, where do we meet?

📝 Make our plan card

A short plan in six boxes that everyone at home should know. Press the button to fill it in and get a card to keep on every phone; empty boxes print as lines to write on.

🔒 What you type stays on this device and is never sent anywhere. Don't add ID numbers or more personal data than you need.

The card's six boxes, and the sources

  1. 🏫 The shelter or evacuation point near home
    Know where it is and the way there, and tell everyone at home
    Sources: Government Public Relations Department (PRD) (2026) · Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) (2022)
  2. 📍 Our family meeting place
    A place everyone knows and can find easily
    Sources: Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
  3. 📞 If we get separated, who do we contact
    Agree how you'll reach each other
    Sources: Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
  4. 🤝 Who helps whom
    Move older and bedridden people to safety first, with their medicine and equipment
    Sources: Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) (2022) · Department of Health (DOH)
  5. 🤝 Who helps whom (another)
  6. 💊 Medicine and must-haves
    Daily medicine for at least 7 days
    Sources: Ministry of Public Health (MOPH) (2013) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency

Thai emergency helplines

  • 1784 DDPM disaster hotline: Report disasters, ask for help, 24 h
  • 199 Fire: A fire you can't put out: get out, then call the fire service
  • 1669 Medical emergency (NIEMS): Ambulance, 24 h
  • 1555 Bangkok (BMA): Report problems and get help in Bangkok
  • 1130 MEA electricity: Bangkok, Nonthaburi, Samut Prakan
  • 1129 PEA electricity: The other 74 provinces
  • 1323 Mental health hotline: Stressed or worn out after the flood? Free, 24 h
