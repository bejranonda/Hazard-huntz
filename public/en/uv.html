What to do at each UV index level

The UV index says how strongly the sun can burn skin and eyes. It peaks around midday, and can be high on a cloudy or cool day. The Thai Meteorological Department forecasts it for each province every day (for a clear sky).

📍 What is it like at your subdistrict now? See Thai Risk

  • Low (0–2)
    • No special protection needed: you can be outside as usual ⚠️
    Sources: World Health Organization (WHO): 2002, 2022 · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
  • Moderate to high (3–7): protection needed
    • Around midday, keep to the shade ⚠️
    • Out in the sun: long sleeves, sunscreen and a hat ⚠️
    • Wear sunglasses too ⚠️
    Sources: World Health Organization (WHO): 2002, 2022 · Department of Health (DOH): 2015 · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
  • Very high (8–10): extra protection
    • Around midday, avoid being outdoors
    • If you must go out, always find shade
    • Long sleeves, sunscreen and a wide-brimmed hat are a must
    • Wear sunglasses to protect your eyes
    • Keep children and people with sensitive skin out of the midday sun
    Sources: World Health Organization (WHO): 2002, 2022 · Thai Meteorological Department (TMD): 2024 · Ministry of Public Health (MOPH): 2023 · Department of Health (DOH): 2015
  • Extreme (11+): extra protection
    • Around midday, avoid being outdoors
    • If you must go out, always find shade
    • Long sleeves, sunscreen and a wide-brimmed hat are a must
    • Wear sunglasses to protect your eyes
    • Keep children and people with sensitive skin out of the midday sun
    • If you really must go out, keep your time in the sun short
    Sources: World Health Organization (WHO): 2002, 2022 · Thai Meteorological Department (TMD): 2024 · Ministry of Public Health (MOPH): 2023 · Department of Health (DOH): 2015
  • Sunscreen is not a reason to stay in the sun longer ⚠️
    Sources: World Health Organization (WHO): 2002, 2022 · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
  • Clouds or a cool day don't stop UV ⚠️
    Sources: World Health Organization (WHO): 2022 · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
