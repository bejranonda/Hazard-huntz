Storms, strong wind and lightning: readying the house and staying safe

BeforeDuringAfter

☎️ Free calls17841991669

Before

  1. Follow Met Department storm warnings and act on them
    Sources: Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) · Thai Meteorological Department (TMD) · Thai Meteorological Department (TMD) (2023) · Metropolitan Electricity Authority (MEA) (2022) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026)
  2. Check the roof, doors and windows are secure
    Sources: Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) · Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) · Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) (2018)
  3. Put away anything the wind could blow about
    Sources: Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) · Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) (2018)
  4. Trim weak branches on big trees near the house
    Sources: Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) · Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) · Metropolitan Electricity Authority (MEA) (2022) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026)

During

  1. Stay in a sturdy building with doors and windows shut
    Sources: Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) · Department of Disease Control (DDC) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026) · US National Weather Service (NWS)
  2. Don't shelter under big trees, billboards or power poles
    Sources: Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) · Thai Meteorological Department (TMD) (2023) · Department of Disease Control (DDC) · Metropolitan Electricity Authority (MEA) (2025) · US National Weather Service (NWS)
  3. In lightning, keep off rooftops, balconies and open ground
    Sources: Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) · Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) (2018) · Department of Disease Control (DDC) · US National Weather Service (NWS) · US National Weather Service (NWS)
  4. In lightning, don't use anything plugged into the mains
    Sources: Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) · Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) (2018) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026) · US National Weather Service (NWS)

After

  1. Keep away from fallen lines or poles; call 1130 or 1129
    Sources: Provincial Electricity Authority (PEA) (2026) · Provincial Electricity Authority (PEA) (2025) · Provincial Electricity Authority (PEA) · Metropolitan Electricity Authority (MEA) (2022) · Metropolitan Electricity Authority (MEA) (2026) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026)
  2. After the storm, check electrics and repair any damage
    Sources: Metropolitan Electricity Authority (MEA) (2022) · Metropolitan Electricity Authority (MEA) (2025) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency

Thai emergency helplines

  • 1784 DDPM disaster hotline: Report disasters, ask for help, 24 h
  • 199 Fire: A fire you can't put out: get out, then call the fire service
  • 1669 Medical emergency (NIEMS): Ambulance, 24 h
  • 1555 Bangkok (BMA): Report problems and get help in Bangkok
  • 1130 MEA electricity: Bangkok, Nonthaburi, Samut Prakan
  • 1129 PEA electricity: The other 74 provinces
  • 1323 Mental health hotline: Stressed or worn out after the flood? Free, 24 h
