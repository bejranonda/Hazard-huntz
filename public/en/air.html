The 5 colours, what to do, and the sources

  1. Blue · very good · PM2.5 0–15.0 µg/m³ · AQI 0–25
    Everyone: Go about life as normal
    Sources: Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023) · Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023)
  2. Green · good · PM2.5 15.1–25.0 µg/m³ · AQI 26–50
    Everyone: Outdoor activities as normal
    People at risk: Watch for symptoms: coughing a lot, hard breathing, wheezing, a tight chest, a racing heart, dizziness
    Sources: Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023) · Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023)
  3. Yellow · moderate · PM2.5 25.1–37.5 µg/m³ · AQI 51–100
    Everyone: Do less hard exercise or work outdoors
    People at risk: Wear a PM2.5 mask every time you go out · Do less hard exercise or work outdoors · If you feel unwell, talk to a doctor soon
    Sources: Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023) · Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023)
  4. Orange · starting to affect health · PM2.5 37.6–75.0 µg/m³ · AQI 101–200
    Everyone: Wear a PM2.5 mask every time you go out · Keep hard exercise or work outdoors short · Watch for symptoms such as coughing, hard breathing or sore eyes
    People at risk: Wear a PM2.5 mask every time you go out · Avoid hard exercise or work outdoors · Follow your doctor's advice; if you feel unwell, see a doctor soon
    Long-term illness: Have your medicine and equipment ready — Department of Health (DOH)
    Sources: Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023) · Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023) · Department of Health (DOH) (2023)
  5. Red · affects health · PM2.5 75.1+ µg/m³ · AQI 201+
    Everyone: No outdoor activities · If you must go out, always wear a PM2.5 mask · If you feel unwell, see a doctor soon · Stay in a room that keeps the dust out — Department of Health (DOH)
    Long-term illness: Stay in a room that keeps the dust out, with your medicine and equipment ready, and follow your doctor's advice closely
    Sources: Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023) · Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023) · Department of Health (DOH) (2023)
  6. 👶 Children under 2 shouldn’t wear any kind of mask: keep them in a room that keeps the dust out, and away from dusty places
    Sources: Department of Disease Control (DDC) (2025) · Department of Health (DOH) (2026) · Public Health Agency of Canada (2026) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency
