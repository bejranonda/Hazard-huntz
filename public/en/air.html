The 5 colours, what to do, and the sources

  1. Blue · very good · PM2.5 0–15.0 µg/m³ · AQI 0–25
    Everyone: Everyone can go about life as normal
    Sources: Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023) · Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023)
  2. Green · good · PM2.5 15.1–25.0 µg/m³ · AQI 26–50
    Everyone: Outdoor activities as normal
    People at risk: Watch for unusual symptoms: frequent cough, hard breathing, wheeze, a tight chest, palpitations, dizziness
    Sources: Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023) · Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023)
  3. Yellow · moderate · PM2.5 25.1–37.5 µg/m³ · AQI 51–100
    Everyone: Spend less time on hard outdoor activity or exercise
    People at risk: Wear a PM2.5 mask every time you go outside · Spend less time on hard outdoor activity or exercise · Any unusual symptoms: see a doctor promptly
    Sources: Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023) · Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023)
  4. Orange · starting to affect health · PM2.5 37.6–75.0 µg/m³ · AQI 101–200
    Everyone: Wear a PM2.5 mask every time you go outside · Limit hard outdoor activity or exercise · Watch for symptoms such as a cough, hard breathing or sore eyes
    People at risk: Wear a PM2.5 mask every time you go outside · Avoid hard outdoor activity or exercise · Follow the doctor's advice; any unusual symptoms, see a doctor promptly
    Long-term illness: Have the medicine and equipment you need ready — Department of Health (DOH)
    Sources: Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023) · Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023) · Department of Health (DOH) (2023)
  5. Red · affects health · PM2.5 75.1+ µg/m³ · AQI 201+
    Everyone: No outdoor activities · If you must go out, always wear a PM2.5 mask · Any unusual symptoms: see a doctor promptly · Stay in a dust-free room — Department of Health (DOH)
    Long-term illness: Stay in a room kept safe from air pollution, with the medicine and equipment you need ready, and follow the doctor's advice strictly
    Sources: Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023) · Pollution Control Department (PCD) (2023) · Department of Health (DOH) (2023)
