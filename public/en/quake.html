Earthquakes: readying the home, and what to do during and after

BeforeDuringAfter

☎️ Free calls1784disasters1669ambulance199fire

Before

  1. Fix heavy appliances firmly to the wall or floor
    Sources: Department of Mineral Resources (DMR) (2025) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026)
  2. Keep heavy things off high shelves and ledges
    Sources: Department of Mineral Resources (DMR) (2025)
  3. Keep a torch, spare batteries and a first-aid kit at home
    Sources: Department of Mineral Resources (DMR) (2025) · Department of Health (DOH) (2023) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026)

During

  1. Drop, cover and hold on under a sturdy table
    Sources: Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) (2025) · Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) (2019) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026)
  2. Keep away from glass doors and windows
    Sources: Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) (2019) · Department of Health (DOH) (2023) · Department of Mineral Resources (DMR) (2025) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026)
  3. By the sea: a quake or a sudden ebb? Go to high ground
    Sources: Department of Mineral Resources (DMR) (2025) · Thai Meteorological Department (TMD) · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA) (2026) · ⚠️ being reviewed with the agency

After

  1. Leave by the fire stairs, never the lift
    Sources: Department of Mineral Resources (DMR) (2025) · Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) (2019) · Department of Health (DOH) (2023)
  2. Don't rush back in; wait for a check or official word
    Sources: Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) (2025) · Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) (2019)
  3. Smell gas? Shut the cylinder valve, open doors and windows
    Sources: Department of Mineral Resources (DMR) (2025)
  4. Wear closed shoes against broken glass and debris
    Sources: Department of Mineral Resources (DMR) (2025)

Thai emergency helplines

  • 1784 DDPM disaster hotline: Report disasters, ask for help, 24 h
  • 199 Fire: A fire you can't put out: get out, then call the fire service
  • 1669 Medical emergency (NIEMS): Ambulance, 24 h
  • 1555 Bangkok (BMA): Report problems and get help in Bangkok
  • 1130 MEA electricity: Bangkok, Nonthaburi, Samut Prakan
  • 1129 PEA electricity: The other 74 provinces
  • 1323 Mental health hotline: Stressed or worn out after the flood? Free, 24 h
