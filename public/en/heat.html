Extreme heat

The baby's asleep in the car ลูกหลับอยู่ในรถ

Front of the house · 🌡️ Heat

Do this: Take the baby with you; never leave anyone in the car, even briefly

Never leave a child, or anyone, in a car parked in the sun, even briefly.

Why: The Department of Disease Control warns that a car parked in the sun without air conditioning heats up very fast, within 10–20 minutes: never leave anyone in it, above all young children. The Department of Health and WHO say the same.

Sources: Department of Disease Control (DDC), news report: 18 Mar 2025, 20 Mar 2025 · Department of Health (DOH), news report: 2023 · World Health Organization (WHO): 2026

Sweeping the yard at noon จะกวาดลานบ้านตอนเที่ยง

Front of the house · 🌡️ Heat

Do this: Leave it for the morning or evening; rest in the shade for now

Fierce midday sun? Move outdoor work to the morning or evening.

Why: The Department of Health says choose activities indoors or in the shade with good airflow, and do outdoor activity in the morning and evening. The DDC says avoid long spells in strong sun, and Bangkok's environment department says cut outdoor activity in the middle of the day once the heat index reaches the warning level.

Sources: Department of Health (DOH), news report: 2023 · Department of Disease Control (DDC), news report: 2025 · Bangkok Metropolitan Administration (BMA), news report: 2026 · World Health Organization (WHO): 2026

Football in the afternoon sun เด็กๆ จะเตะบอลกลางแดดบ่าย

Front of the house · 🌡️ Heat

Do this: Let them play in the cooler evening, with water to sip often

Kids playing outside? Morning or evening, with water to sip often.

Why: The Department of Disease Control lists children among those most at risk from heat, and says drink clean water often and avoid long spells in the sun. The Department of Health says do outdoor activity in the morning and evening, and that children need special care.

Sources: Department of Disease Control (DDC), news report: 2025 · Department of Health (DOH), news report: 2023 · World Health Organization (WHO): 2026

Grandma barely drinks ยายไม่ค่อยดื่มน้ำ

Living room · 🌡️ Heat

Do this: Put water within her reach and get her to sip often, without waiting for thirst

Keep water by older people and get them sipping before they feel thirsty.

Why: The Department of Health says carers should keep clean water by older people, such as a glass on the bedside table, use a cup with a handle or a straw, and get them drinking regularly without waiting for thirst; avoid tea, coffee, alcohol and sugary drinks. People with a chronic illness drink the amount their doctor advises.

Sources: Department of Health (DOH), news report: 2022 · Department of Disease Control (DDC), news report: 2025 · Bangkok Metropolitan Administration (BMA), news report: 2026

The fan at Grandpa's face พัดลมจ่อหน้าตาทั้งคืน

Living room · 🌡️ Heat

Do this: Set the fan back from him, on oscillate, not blowing straight at him

Fan for an older person? Set it back, on oscillate, never straight at them.

Why: The Department of Health says older people are best in a home or building with air conditioning; without it, use a fan set away from the body, oscillating, not blowing straight at them, and keep the air moving. The DDC says avoid hot, stuffy places with no airflow.

Sources: Department of Health (DOH), news report: 2022 · Department of Disease Control (DDC), news report: 2025

Uncle: very hot and confused ลุงตัวร้อนจัด พูดจาสับสน

Living room · 🌡️ Heat

Do this: Call 1669, move him into shade, loosen his clothes, cool him with a wet cloth

Very hot, confused or drowsy? Call 1669, get them into shade, cool them down.

Why: The Department of Health says watch for heatstroke: a very hot body, confusion, red skin, no sweating; the Department of Medical Services adds that early on a person can still sweat. The Department of Disease Control says move them into shade, loosen their clothes, wipe the body, neck, armpits and head with a cloth soaked in cool water, with a fan; if they are unconscious, lay them on their side; then rush them to hospital or call 1669.

Sources: Department of Disease Control (DDC), news report: 18 Mar 2025, 20 Mar 2025 · Department of Health (DOH), news report: 2023, 2026 · Department of Medical Services (DMS), news report: 2023 · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA): 2026

No idea how hot tomorrow will be ไม่รู้ว่าพรุ่งนี้จะร้อนระดับไหน

Living room · 🌡️ Heat

Do this: Check the heat index warning first, then plan around the hottest hours

Going out? Check the heat index warning and avoid the hottest hours.

Why: The heat index is the temperature the body feels from both air temperature and humidity, and the Thai Meteorological Department publishes it. Bangkok's environment department says follow heat warnings; at the warning level cut outdoor activity in the middle of the day, and at extreme danger stop it altogether.

Sources: Thai Meteorological Department (TMD), news report: 2024 · Bangkok Metropolitan Administration (BMA), news report: 2026

A thick black top เสื้อแขนยาวสีดำตัวหนา

Living room · 🌡️ Heat

Do this: Change into something light and loose; take a hat, an umbrella and water

Out in the heat? Light, loose clothes, a hat, an umbrella and water.

Why: The Department of Disease Control says wear clothes that let heat out, light colours, not tight, and avoid dark colours. The Department of Health says older people should avoid going out in extreme heat; if they must, a hat, light-coloured long sleeves, loose trousers, an umbrella and water.

Sources: Department of Disease Control (DDC), news report: 18 Mar 2025, 20 Mar 2025 · Department of Health (DOH), news report: 2022 · Ready.gov (U.S. FEMA): 2026

An ice-cold beer for the heat เบียร์เย็นๆ แก้ร้อน

Kitchen · 🌡️ Heat

Do this: Drink clean water instead; no alcohol on very hot days

On very hot days, skip alcohol; sip clean water often instead.

Why: The Department of Disease Control says avoid alcohol, drink clean water often, and an electrolyte drink if you sweat a lot. The Department of Health also says older people should avoid alcohol, tea, coffee and sugary drinks in hot weather.

Sources: Department of Disease Control (DDC), news report: 18 Mar 2025, 20 Mar 2025 · Department of Health (DOH), news report: 2022 · U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC): 2026

Cramp after fixing the roof in the sun ตะคริวหลังซ่อมหลังคากลางแดด

Kitchen · 🌡️ Heat

Do this: Rest in the shade and sip clean water or an electrolyte drink

Sweating hard or cramping? Rest in the shade; sip water or electrolytes.

Why: The Department of Disease Control says drink clean water often, an electrolyte drink if you sweat a lot, and avoid long spells in strong sun. The Department of Health's material says heat cramps call for clean water or an electrolyte drink, and heat exhaustion for rest in a cool place with loosened clothing.

Sources: Department of Disease Control (DDC), news report: 2025 · Department of Health (DOH), news report: 2026
